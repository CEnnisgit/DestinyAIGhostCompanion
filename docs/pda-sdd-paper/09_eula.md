# §4.3.6: End User License Agreement (EULA)

The EULA serves as a foundational legal document for any software system. The PDA-SDD model proposes a standardized structure encompassing the essential components.

## Structure

```
EULA
├── 1. Grant of License
├── 2. Ownership
├── 3. Restrictions
├── 4. Warranty Disclaimer
├── 5. Indemnification
├── 6. Termination
├── 7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction
├── 8. Entire Agreement
└── 9. Severability
```

## Nine Essential Sections

| # | Section | Description |
|---|---------|-------------|
| 1 | **Grant of License** | Terms under which the software is licensed |
| 2 | **Ownership** | Intellectual property rights |
| 3 | **Restrictions** | Usage limitations |
| 4 | **Warranty Disclaimer** | Limitations on warranty |
| 5 | **Indemnification** | Liability protections |
| 6 | **Termination** | Conditions for agreement termination |
| 7 | **Governing Law & Jurisdiction** | Applicable legal framework |
| 8 | **Entire Agreement** | Primacy of this agreement |
| 9 | **Severability** | Handling of invalid provisions |

> **Note:** Specific content and emphasis can differ depending on the nature of the software, the parties involved, and applicable legal jurisdiction. It is strongly recommended that EULAs be reviewed and approved by legal professionals.
